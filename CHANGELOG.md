# Changelog

## 0.2.0

Added optional offline eSpeak narration, user-provided background music, FFmpeg burned subtitles, eight basic environment drawings, and storyboard contact sheets. Expanded automated tests to cover narration and storyboard generation.

## 0.1.0

Initial deterministic procedural video generator with scene caching and MP4 export.

## 0.3.0

Added offline screenplay/dialogue parsing, persistent named speakers, pitch-separated local TTS, contextual environments and chart props, articulated elbows and knees, camera composition variants, WebVTT captions, audio limiter, and extended 30-second multi-scene resume tests. Full 30-minute rendering and live agent invocation remain unverified.
